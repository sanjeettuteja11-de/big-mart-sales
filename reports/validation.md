## How to read it

- **Holdout vs K-fold**: a single 20% split has a standard error of roughly the fold std, so it can rank two close models the wrong way; the repeated K-fold number is the one to trust.
- **Grouped by product**: validation products are unseen. Every test product appears in train, so this is not the competition's scenario; it shows how much each model relies on product identity.
- **Preprocessing fit on train only / refit in fold**: the default fits imputation and aggregate features on train and test *features* together (no sales). If these rows match the default, that choice is harmless.
- **Negative predictions**: predictions are clipped at zero, which can only reduce squared error. The share tells how often it matters.
